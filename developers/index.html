What you can publish

If it runs in a web browser, it can run on UNTILS.

HTML5 games

Plain JavaScript, Canvas, WebGL and WebAssembly games, and frameworks such as Phaser, Three.js or PixiJS.

Engine exports

Web exports from Unity, Cocos Creator and Godot work too, as long as the export has an index.html at the top.

Mobile games

Choose Mobile game when your game plays well on phones. It opens fullscreen on a phone and in a 16:9 frame on a computer.

Desktop games

Need a keyboard or a mouse? Choose Desktop game. Phone players will see that it is made for computers.

Your game page, your way

Every game gets its own page. Fill it well and players understand your game before they press Play.

What you can add

  • Name and description. Write as much as you like, up to 2000 words. Tell the story, the goal and what makes your game fun.
  • How to play. Explain the controls and the goal in up to 500 words.
  • Categories. Pick 1 to 5 categories so players can find your game.
  • Icon. A square picture, at least 512 x 512 px (1024 x 1024 is best).
  • Screenshots and more. Up to 5 screenshots, plus an optional thumbnail, banner and video.
  • Device and screen. Say if the game is for phones and computers, or for computers only, and whether it needs portrait or landscape.

What players see

  • Your studio name on the game page, with a "More from this developer" row.
  • A Play button that starts the game right in the browser, with fullscreen on phones.
  • Likes, comments, favourites and play counts, so you can see what players enjoy.
  • Your game on the home page, in its category pages and in the "More games" list.
  • A clean, fast page that works on phones, tablets and computers.

Before you upload: a quick checklist

Most games are approved the first time when these points are in place.

The game package

  • One .zip file, up to 50 MB. We recommend under 20 MB so it loads fast on mobile data.
  • index.html is at the top of the zip, not inside a sub-folder.
  • Files use relative paths such as game.js or img/hero.png.
  • The game starts by itself from the Play screen. No login, no installs, no pop-ups.
  • It fills the window, keeps its shape and never needs scrolling.

Content and quality

  • You made the game, or you have permission to use every picture, sound, font and piece of code.
  • No malware, hidden tracking or code that could harm players.
  • No copied characters, logos or brands, and nothing illegal, hateful or sexual.
  • It does not ask players for personal information.
  • It loads quickly, runs smoothly and the buttons are easy to tap on a phone.

How the review works

Every game is checked by a real person on our team. Here is what happens after you press Upload.

  1. You submitWe email you to confirm that your game was received. Nothing else is needed from you.
  2. We reviewWe open your game on phone and computer, check that it works and that it follows our rules.
  3. You get the resultWithin 2 days you get an email. If a change is needed, we tell you exactly what to fix.
  4. Your game goes liveApproved games are published for players. You can see the status any time in your dashboard.

Updating a live game? Upload a new version from your dashboard. The current version stays live while we review the new one.

Tips for a quick approval

Small things that make a big difference.

Make it feel ready

  • Test on a real phone and on a computer before you upload.
  • Add the viewport tag and make the game fill the window.
  • Choose the right device and screen direction, so players get the best view.
  • Use the starter game from our documentation to see how publishing works.

Make it easy to find

  • Use a short, clear game name and a bright, simple icon that looks good very small.
  • Write a real description with the goal, the story and the controls.
  • Add screenshots that show the real game with all its buttons.
  • Pick every category that really fits, up to five.

Questions developers ask

Cannot find your answer? Write to us and a real person will reply.

Does it cost anything to publish a game?

No. Creating a developer account and publishing games on UNTILS is free.

How long does the review take?

We reply within 2 days, by email. If your game needs a change, the email explains what to fix, and you can upload a new version.

Which engines and tools can I use?

Any tool that produces an HTML5 game. Unity, Cocos Creator and Godot web exports work as long as the zip has index.html at the top and stays under 50 MB.

Can I update my game after it is live?

Yes. Upload a new version from your dashboard. Players keep playing the current version while we review the new one.

Do I need to be a company?

No. Solo developers and small teams are welcome. Your studio name is simply the name shown on your game.

Who owns the game?

You do. You give UNTILS the right to host, show and promote your game while it is on the site. You can ask us to remove it at any time. See the Terms of Use for details.

Does my game need to work on phones?

It does not have to. Choose Desktop game if it needs a keyboard or mouse. Phone players will see that it is made for computers.

Where can I report a problem with the portal?

Use the bug report form on our Contact page. It collects the details we need to fix things quickly.
