Quick start

  1. Make your game as a normal HTML5 game with an index.html.
  2. (Recommended) Download the SDK below and add it to your game.
  3. Put everything in one .zip file. index.html must be at the top level of the zip.
  4. Open your dashboard, press + ADD NEW GAME, fill in the details, add your icon and zip, and press UPLOAD GAME.
  5. Our team reviews it (up to 2 days). After approval we make it live on UNTILS.

Game package (the zip)

ItemRule
FormatOne .zip file. index.html at the top (not inside a sub-folder).
Max size50 MB. We recommend under 20 MB so the game loads fast on mobile data.
File pathsUse relative paths: game.js, img/hero.png. Do not start paths with /, because your game runs inside /live/....
TechnologyHTML5, JavaScript, Canvas, WebGL, WebAssembly. Web exports from Unity, Godot and Cocos work as long as the export has an index.html (engine plugins for the SDK are coming later).
Starts by itselfNo login, no installs, no pop-ups. The game must start from the Play screen.
Good to know: your game runs in a protected sandbox. It cannot read UNTILS accounts or cookies. Browser localStorage is saved for you automatically, and the SDK gives you a cleaner way to save (see below).

Device: mobile or desktop

When you upload, choose Mobile game if it plays well on phones and PC. Choose Desktop game if it needs a keyboard or mouse. Desktop games show "This game is for desktop (PC) only" on phones instead of a Play button.

Recommended game size (what the player sees)

WhereWhat to do
PCDesign for 1280 x 720 (16:9 landscape). The game area is shown in a 16:9 frame and can go fullscreen.
PhoneWhen the player presses Play, the game opens fullscreen. Support portrait (720 x 1280) or landscape, and tell players if one is required.

Most important: make the game responsive. It must fill the window (window.innerWidth x window.innerHeight), keep its aspect ratio, and never need scrolling. Add this to your page:

<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width,initial-scale=1,viewport-fit=cover">
<style>html,body{margin:0;height:100%;overflow:hidden;touch-action:manipulation}</style>

Game icon (logo) - required

ItemRule
ShapeSquare (width = height).
SizeMinimum 512 x 512 px. We recommend 1024 x 1024 px. Smaller or non-square icons are rejected by the upload form.
FormatPNG, JPG or WebP. Under 2 MB.

What to put in it:

  • One clear subject: your main character, object or logo mark, in the centre.
  • Keep about 10% empty space around the edges. UNTILS rounds the corners itself, so do not round them.
  • Bright, high-contrast colours and a solid background. It must look good very small (about 60 px).
  • Little or no text. Tiny text becomes unreadable. A short game name in large letters is fine.
  • No borders, no store badges, no "NEW" or "FREE" stickers, no blurry or stretched images.
  • Only artwork you made or are allowed to use. No copied characters or logos.

The icon is shown on the game card, the Play screen, "More games" and the popular banner.

Popular banner - optional

If the UNTILS team marks your game as Popular, it appears in the big banner at the top of the home page. A banner is optional, but without one your game shows only its icon on a gradient.

ItemRule
Size1920 x 400 px (ratio about 4.8 : 1). Minimum 1600 px wide.
FormatPNG, JPG or WebP. Under 2 MB.
Safe areaThe banner is cropped on small screens. Keep important art in the centre 1000 x 190 px.
TextDo not write the game name or a Play button on it. UNTILS adds the title and a Play Now button on the left, with a dark fade for readability. Keep the left side calm.

How to upload: choose it in the Popular banner field when you add the game. You can also add or change it later: open your dashboard, click the game, pick a new banner, and press SAVE CHANGES.

Screenshots - optional

ItemRule
CountUp to 5.
SizeMinimum 640 px wide. We recommend 1280 x 720 (landscape) or 720 x 1280 (portrait for phone games).
FormatPNG, JPG or WebP. Under 2 MB each.

What to show: real in-game screens (menu, gameplay, a level, a win or upgrade screen). No fake or edited-in screens, no device frames, no big promo text, no screens from other games.

Screenshots appear at the very bottom of your game page. Click one to see it large. You can remove or add screenshots later from the dashboard.

Review and going live

  1. PENDING - your game is waiting. Our team plays it on PC and phone and writes reviews. Review takes up to 2 days.
  2. APPROVED or REJECTED - the leader decides. If rejected, a note explains why on your dashboard, and you can fix it and upload again.
  3. LIVE - the game is public on UNTILS. A green PLAY button appears next to it in your dashboard.
Updating a live game: click it in your dashboard to change the description, category, icon, banner, screenshots, or upload a new version. When you press SAVE CHANGES the update goes live right away. We can take a game offline if an update breaks the rules.

SDK for HTML5 games

The SDK is one small file, untils.js. It gives your game progress saving, pause/resume, and (soon) ads and leaderboards. It is optional, but recommended. Games that use it will keep working as we add features.

Import

  1. Download untils.js.
  2. Put it next to your index.html inside your game folder (and so inside the zip).
  3. Add this in your page before your own game script:
<script src="untils.js"></script>

Using a local copy means your game also runs on your own PC without UNTILS. In that case the SDK switches to offline mode: saves go to your browser and ads are skipped, so nothing crashes.

Full flow

async function main() {
  await Untils.init();               // 1. always first

  Untils.loadingStart();             // 2. assets start loading
  await loadAssets();
  Untils.loadingFinished();          // 3. menu is ready

  let level = await Untils.load("level", 1);   // 4. read saved progress

  playBtn.onclick = () => {
    Untils.gameplayStart();          // 5. player starts playing
  };

  function onGameOver() {
    Untils.gameplayStop();           // 6. player died / opened menu
    Untils.save("level", level);     // 7. save progress
    Untils.showAd("interstitial");   // 8. ad between levels (not live yet)
  }

  rewardBtn.onclick = async () => {
    const watched = await Untils.showAd("rewarded");   // 9. watch ad for a reward
    if (watched) giveCoins();
  };

  Untils.onPause(() => pauseGame());     // tab hidden / app switched
  Untils.onResume(() => resumeGame());
}
main();

SDK commands

CommandWhat it doesStatus
await Untils.init()Starts the SDK. Resolves with { env, isMobile, player, version }. env is "untils" on the site or "offline" on your PC. Never fails.Live
loadingStart() / loadingFinished()Tell UNTILS when your game is loading and when it is ready.Live
gameplayStart() / gameplayStop()Call when real play starts and when it stops (death, menu, pause). Used for stats and, later, ad timing.Live
Untils.save(key, value)Saves any JSON value (number, text, object, array). Example: Untils.save("coins", 120).Live (this browser)
await Untils.load(key, fallback)Reads it back. Returns fallback if nothing is saved.Live (this browser)
Untils.remove(key) / Untils.clearSave()Delete one value / all values.Live
Untils.onPause(fn) / onResume(fn)Run your function when the player leaves or returns to the tab. Pause your game here.Live
await Untils.showAd("interstitial" | "rewarded")Interstitial resolves true to continue. Rewarded resolves true only if the player earned the reward. Until ads go live: interstitial returns true at once, rewarded returns false.Coming soon
await Untils.getPlayer()Returns { guest, name }. Everyone is "Guest" for now.Basic
await Untils.submitScore(board, score)Sends a score. Returns false until leaderboards go live. Safe to call now.Coming soon
Untils.isMobile, Untils.env, Untils.isReadyHandy flags.Live
Saving tips: save small things (level, coins, settings) often, not huge data. Today saves stay in the player's browser on that device. When player accounts arrive, the same save and load commands will work on every device, so your game will not need changes.

Rules

  • Your game must be yours, or you must have the right to publish it.
  • No adult content, hate, or violence aimed at real people.
  • No malware, crypto-mining, tracking, or collecting players' personal data.
  • No misleading names, icons, screenshots or banners.
  • The game must work, load in a reasonable time, and not crash.
