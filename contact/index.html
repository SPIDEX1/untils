SEND US A MESSAGE OR REPORT A BUG

Fill in the form and press the button. Your email app opens with everything written for you, so you only have to press send. Nothing is sent until you do that.

WHAT TO INCLUDE IN A BUG REPORT

  • The name of the game, or the page address (URL) where it happened.
  • Your phone or computer and the browser you use, for example "Android phone, Chrome".
  • What you tapped or clicked, step by step, and what you saw.
  • A screenshot, if you can. You can attach it in your email app.
  • Whether it happens every time or only sometimes.

COMMON QUESTIONS

A game does not start or the screen stays black

Reload the page, check your internet connection and try another browser. Some games are made for computers only and say so on their page. If it still fails, send us a bug report with the game name and your device.

My progress or favourites are missing

Progress is saved in your browser, so clearing browser data removes it. Sign in with the same account to get your favourites back on any device.

I am a developer and my game is waiting

Every game is reviewed within 2 days and you get the result by email, with a clear reason if something needs a change. Check your dashboard for the status. Write to us if it takes longer.

I want my account or data deleted

Choose "Privacy request" in the form and tell us the email address of your account. See our Privacy Policy for your rights.

I think a game or a comment breaks the rules

Choose "Report a game or a comment" and send us the game name and what is wrong. We review every report.

Copyright complaints

Choose "Copyright or takedown" and include the details listed in our Terms of Use, such as the page address and proof that you own the work.
