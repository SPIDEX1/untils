1. WHO WE ARE

UNTILS is run from India by a small independent team. For the purposes of this policy we are the party that decides how your information is used on the site. You can reach us at any time at untilsofficial07@gmail.com or through the Contact page.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

  • Information you give us. If you contact us or send a bug report, we receive what you write, such as your name, email address, the message and any details you add about your device.
  • Player accounts. If you sign in as a player (with Google or with an email code), we store your email address, the name and picture that Google provides, and your sign-in session. If you choose a username or a profile picture, we store those too. We also store your favourite games and recently played games so they follow you from one device to another. Sign-in is handled by our login provider, Supabase.
  • Studio or developer name. When you sign up we ask for a studio or developer name. It is saved on your account. The same name is used for your developer account, so one login works on both parts of the site.
  • Developer accounts. If you use the developer portal, we also store the games, descriptions, images, videos and zip files you upload, their review status and any notes our team writes to you.
  • Comments and likes. If you write a comment, we store the text, the time, your display name and your picture. Likes and dislikes are stored against a random visitor ID.
  • Information stored on your device. We use your browser's local storage and session storage to keep you signed in, remember your game likes, keep your game progress and hold a random visitor ID. The visitor ID is not linked to your name or email. It lets us count plays and likes once per visitor. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.
  • Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) and our servers receive technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and the date and time of your visit. We use this to run the site, keep it secure, prevent abuse and understand which games are popular.

We do not ask for payment details, because every game on UNTILS is free. We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information such as health data, precise location or government ID numbers.

3. COOKIES AND LOCAL STORAGE

Cookies and local storage are small pieces of data saved on your device. We use them for these purposes:

  • Strictly necessary. Keeping you signed in, including one small sign-in cookie shared by untils.in and developer.untils.in so that one login works on both. Protecting the site from abuse.
  • Preferences and progress. Remembering your likes, favourites, language choice and saved game progress.
  • Advertising. Our advertising partners, including Google, use cookies and similar technologies to show and measure ads (see section 4).

You can block or delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Some parts of the site, such as sign-in and saved progress, may not work without them.

4. ADVERTISING AND GOOGLE ADSENSE

UNTILS shows ads provided by third parties, including Google AdSense. Ads help us keep the games free. Ads are labelled and are kept separate from game content.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our site or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Google may use the DoubleClick advertising cookie, and similar identifiers such as mobile advertising IDs, to show and measure ads.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. To learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps and Google's advertising technologies.
  • Other third-party vendors and ad networks may also place and read cookies on your browser, or use web beacons, to serve ads on our site. You can opt out of many of them at aboutads.info, optout.networkadvertising.org or on the vendor's own website. The ad vendor we use is Google AdSense (google.com).
  • On mobile devices you can also limit ad tracking in your device's advertising settings.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, ads are personalised only where the law allows. Where consent is required, it is requested through a consent message provided by Google, and you can change or withdraw your choice at any time. If you do not consent, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised.
  • We do not control cookies set by advertisers and ad networks. Please read their own privacy policies.

We never give advertisers your name, email address or other details that directly identify you. We do not click on our own ads and we ask everyone else not to (see our Terms of Use).

5. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

  • To run the site, show games and keep your account and saved progress working.
  • To answer your messages and bug reports, and to review games that developers submit.
  • To count plays and likes, understand what is popular and improve UNTILS.
  • To keep the site safe, prevent fraud, invalid ad clicks and abuse, and follow the law.
  • To show ads that help pay for the site.

Where the law asks for a legal basis, we rely on: running our service for you (your account and the games), our legitimate interest in keeping the site safe and improving it, your consent where it is required (for example for personalised ads), and legal duties. We do not sell your personal information for money.

6. WHO WE SHARE IT WITH

We share information only with:

  • service providers that help us run UNTILS: Supabase (login, database and file storage), Cloudflare (hosting, storage and security) and Google (sign-in with Google, fonts and ads);
  • advertising partners, as described in section 4;
  • other people, when the law, a court or a government authority requires it, or to protect the rights, safety and security of UNTILS and our users.

If UNTILS is ever sold or merged, information may move to the new owner, who must follow this policy.

7. WHAT IS PUBLIC

Comments you post are public. They show your display name (your username if you set one, otherwise the name from your account) and your picture next to the comment. Your email address is never shown. Developers' studio names are shown on their game pages. Please do not put personal details such as phone numbers or addresses in comments. You can delete your own comments at any time.

8. GAMES FROM OTHER DEVELOPERS

Many games on UNTILS are made by independent developers and run inside a protected frame on our pages. A game cannot read your UNTILS account or your sign-in cookies. Games are reviewed before they go live, and our rules require developers to respect your privacy and not to collect personal information. Each developer is still responsible for their own game. If you see a game that asks for information it should not, please tell us through the Contact page.

9. LANGUAGE AND FONTS

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google servers to fetch them. You can change the language of UNTILS with the language button in the footer. English is the default and loads nothing extra. If you choose another language, the text of the page is translated by Google Translate: a script from Google is loaded, and your choice is saved in a cookie called googtrans so that the next page opens in the same language. Google may process data under its own privacy policy. The small country flags next to the language names are pictures loaded from flagcdn.com. Games from developers are not translated.

10. HOW LONG WE KEEP INFORMATION

  • Account information: while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete it and the data linked to it, except what we must keep by law.
  • Messages and bug reports: for as long as needed to answer them and to fix the problem.
  • Server logs: for a short time, for security and to find faults.
  • Comments: until you or our team delete them, or your account is deleted.
  • Games you upload: until you remove them or we remove them under our terms.

11. SECURITY AND TRANSFERS

We use reasonable technical measures, such as encrypted connections (HTTPS) and limited access, to protect information. No method is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. Our service providers may process data in countries outside your own, including outside India, under their own safeguards.

12. YOUR RIGHTS AND CHOICES

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, the GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state laws), you may have the right to:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your account and your personal data;
  • object to or limit how your data is used, and withdraw consent you gave earlier;
  • make a complaint to your data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, write to untilsofficial07@gmail.com. We may need to check that the request really comes from you. We will reply within a reasonable time, and within the time the law requires. You can also clear your browser storage to remove saved likes, progress and your visitor ID, and manage ad personalisation as described in section 4.

13. NOTICE FOR US RESIDENTS

If you live in a US state with a privacy law (such as California), you can ask us to tell you what personal information we collect, to delete it or to correct it, and you will not be treated worse for asking. We collect the kinds of information listed in section 2: identifiers (such as email address, name and visitor ID), internet activity (such as pages visited and games played) and what you send us. We do not sell personal information for money. Advertising cookies can count as "sharing" or "selling" for targeted advertising under some laws. You can opt out with the links in section 4 and in your browser or device settings. We do not know of any "Do Not Track" standard that all websites follow, so we do not respond to those signals.

14. CHILDREN

UNTILS is a general audience site and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and we do not knowingly show personalised ads to them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has given us personal information, email untilsofficial07@gmail.com and we will delete it. Players under 18 should use the site with a parent's or guardian's permission.

15. LINKS TO OTHER SITES

Our site links to other websites, such as YouTube, Instagram and X. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

16. CHANGES AND CONTACT

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. If we make an important change, we will say so on this page.

UNTILS, India. Questions, requests or complaints about privacy? Email untilsofficial07@gmail.com or use the Contact page.
