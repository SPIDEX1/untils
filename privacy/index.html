PRIVACY POLICY

LAST UPDATED: 2026-10-16

This policy explains what information UNTILS ("we", "us") collects when you use this website and how we use it.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

  • Information you give us. If you contact us, we receive the details you send, such as your name and email address.
  • Information stored on your device. We use your browser's local storage to remember your game likes. This stays on your device.
  • Developer accounts. If you create a developer account, we store your email address and studio name with our login provider (Supabase), and keep you signed in on your device so you do not have to log in again each time.
  • Usage information. We use privacy-friendly analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular. This does not identify you personally.

ADVERTISING

UNTILS may show ads in games. Advertising partners such as Google may use cookies or similar technology to show ads and measure them. You can manage ad personalisation in your Google account settings.

HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We use information to run the site, answer your messages, keep the site secure and improve our games. We do not sell your personal information.

SHARING

We share information only with service providers who help us run UNTILS (for example hosting and analytics partners) and when the law requires it.

YOUR CHOICES

You can clear your browser storage at any time to remove your saved likes. To ask about, correct or delete information you have sent us, write to untilsofficial07@gmail.com.

CHILDREN

UNTILS is a general audience site. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

CHANGES

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

CONTACT
